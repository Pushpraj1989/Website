# Appointment booking (design)

A design for salon appointment booking in the customer app: pick a treatment, a person, a day and a free time, then
confirm. **Not built yet.** It is shown in the salon video as "designed, built on request". Build it when a customer asks.

- `booking.html`: open at 390 × 844. `booking.html?step=done` shows the confirmation.
- What building it involves: services with durations and prices, staff and their working hours, free slots that
  respect bookings and breaks, a console calendar to see and move bookings, reminders, and cancellation rules.
