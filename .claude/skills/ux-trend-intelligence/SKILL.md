# UX Trend Intelligence

## Weekly scan
Research changes since the last scan across W3C/WAI, Apple, Android/Material, Microsoft Inclusive Design, Adobe Spectrum, Figma, Google PAIR, Unicode CLDR, target-country digital standards, established UX research, major product patterns, and high-signal practitioner discussion.

## Never confuse popularity with evidence
Every finding must be classified:
- STANDARD
- RESEARCH
- MARKET_SIGNAL
- EXPERIMENTAL

Then classify trajectory:
- EMERGING
- GROWING
- ESTABLISHED
- DECLINING
- DISPUTED

Then recommend:
- ADOPT
- PILOT
- WATCH
- REJECT

## Adoption questions
- What user problem does this solve?
- Who benefits?
- What accessibility/cognitive cost appears?
- Is this a stable convention or novelty?
- Does it improve task success, efficiency, comprehension, or trust?
- What evidence would falsify the recommendation?

## Output
Produce a dated delta report with sources, implication, confidence, affected skill/reference files, and whether human approval is needed. Do not silently rewrite core standards.
