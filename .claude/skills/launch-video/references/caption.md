# The post caption

Write it with the marketing skills if you have them (marketing:content-creation), then check every claim.

Structure (LinkedIn): 
1. **The question** that the hook asks, in one line.
2. **What it is and the one number or fact** that backs the promise (only if true: "free", "0% commission", "in a day").
3. **What's inside** as 3–4 short arrow lines.
4. **How it works** in 3–4 numbered steps (the same steps as the film).
5. **What it costs the reader honestly** (what is free, what they still pay for).
6. **The link** (real, live), then one ask ("try it and tell me what's missing").
7. 4–5 hashtags, specific not generic.

Rules: plain words, short lines, no emoji walls, no "revolutionary", no invented numbers or testimonials, no claim the film
doesn't also make honestly. Keep the first two lines strong: LinkedIn cuts the post after about 210 characters.
Put the final caption in `STORYBOARD.md` under "Caption".
