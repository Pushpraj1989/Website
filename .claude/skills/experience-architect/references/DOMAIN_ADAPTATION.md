# Domain Adaptation Rules

## Banking / fintech
Prioritize:
- authentication/recovery resilience for financially consequential journeys
- money/value visibility
- consequence clarity
- authorization/approval states
- fraud/security context
- auditability
- role separation
- reconciliation/exception handling
- precise dates/rates/fees
- accessible tables/data density
- prevention of irreversible mistakes

Do not make financial actions "frictionless" when deliberate review is protective.

## Enterprise operations
Prioritize:
- throughput
- density
- keyboard efficiency
- saved filters/views
- bulk actions
- cross-record navigation
- state persistence
- audit/activity history
- exception queues

## Public service / government
Prioritize:
- accessibility
- plain language
- multilingual/local requirements
- identity/trust
- clear eligibility/evidence
- save/resume
- assisted-service pathways
- low-bandwidth resilience where relevant

## Healthcare / other high-impact domains
Treat recommendations and irreversible actions with stronger explanation, confirmation, evidence, and escalation. Apply current domain regulations and specialist review; this skill does not substitute for clinical/legal compliance expertise.

## Social/community products
Prioritize:
- identity/context clarity
- privacy controls
- reporting/blocking/safety pathways
- notification burden
- content creation/recovery
- moderation state transparency where feasible
- avoiding engagement patterns that degrade user control

## Commerce
Prioritize:
- product understanding
- price/fee transparency
- comparison
- cart persistence
- error-resistant checkout
- accessible forms
- order status
- cancellation/returns clarity
