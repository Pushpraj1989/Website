# Discovery Protocol

## Purpose

Collect only the information that changes UX decisions. Do not interrogate the user with a fixed long questionnaire.

## Adaptive sequence

### Gate A — product and outcome
Establish:
- What is being built?
- What primary outcome must the user achieve?
- What business outcome matters?

### Gate B — people
Establish:
- primary user role/cohort
- secondary/edge cohorts
- approximate expertise level
- relevant age band only when it affects cognition, regulation, device assumptions, or accessibility
- accessibility needs already known

Do not infer capability, preferences, literacy, or technical comfort from nationality or age alone.

### Gate C — market and locale
Establish:
- country/region
- supported languages/locales
- RTL/LTR/mixed-direction requirement
- whether one experience is global or market-specific

### Gate D — use context
Establish:
- desktop/mobile/tablet/kiosk/wearable or multimodal
- office/home/public/on-the-go
- frequency of use
- time pressure and interruptions
- connectivity constraints
- input methods

### Gate E — domain and risk
Establish:
- domain
- critical/high-impact decisions
- compliance/regulatory references known to the team
- privacy/security constraints
- roles/permissions/approval structure

### Gate F — existing system
Establish:
- current product vs greenfield
- design system / brand
- frontend framework
- existing reusable components
- analytics/research/support evidence

## Stop asking when

Stop discovery questions and proceed when:
1. the critical journey can be defined;
2. major UX decisions can be made without inventing material facts;
3. remaining unknowns can be safely tracked as assumptions.

## Assumption Register format

| ID | Assumption | UX impact if wrong | Confidence | Validation method |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-01 | Example | High | Low | User interview / analytics |

## Discovery output

Summarize:
- users
- job
- market
- environment
- frequency/expertise
- risk
- accessibility
- technical/design constraints
- success metrics
- assumptions
