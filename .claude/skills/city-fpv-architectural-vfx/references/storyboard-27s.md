# 27-second storyboard (Prototype 01, hybrid)

Source: the production specification supplied by the team (text, October 2026). Timings are
proposals until Gate 2 is approved.

| Scene | Time | Shot | Camera | VFX and light | Audio | Mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 City FPV arrival | 0-5 s | High flight over Muscat, road network followed, dive toward the development | Speed ramp, natural motion blur, banking | Atmospheric haze; no building identity needed yet | Wind and air, soft pad | AI video or city plate (labelled) |

| 04 Golden energy signature | 16-21 s | Champagne-gold ribbon climbs and spirals up the facade | Locked | Windows light in sequence; reflections travel through glass; crown glows | Soft rising chime | Shader on facade mask (deterministic) |
| 05 Grand reveal | 21-27 s | Pull back to show the whole development in its location; logo, project name, CTA | Slow pull-back | Logo as a separate layer; project name; enquiry line | Resolve, quiet tone | Compositing; logo SVG |

## Website hero cut (8 s, proposal)
- 0-1.5 s: end of the FPV approach, dark.
- 1.5-4 s: the assembled building, the roof at the top of frame.
- 4-6 s: the gold wave passes once.
- 6-8 s: clean hero frame with the project name and the Towers logo; loops to 1.5 s.

## Continuity rules
- The building in scene 02 and scene 03 must share a silhouette, floor count, window grid and roof.
- Keyframe every scene boundary and check it against the approved hero frame.
- Scene 03 uses the same lighting direction as scene 02 unless the storyboard changes it.
- Neighbouring buildings come only from the approved location references. Never invent them.
