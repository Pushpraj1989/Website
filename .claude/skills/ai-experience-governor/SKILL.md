# AI Experience Governor

## Mission
Make AI useful without making it appear more reliable, autonomous, or certain than it is.

## Workflow
1. Define the user problem and why AI is justified over deterministic alternatives.
2. Define capability boundaries and known failure modes.
3. Classify autonomy: Inform → Suggest → Draft → Assist → Act.
4. Establish the user's mental model and onboarding needs.
5. Decide what evidence/explanation is needed at decision time.
6. Expose uncertainty only when meaningful and calibrated; never fabricate precision.
7. Provide edit/reject/override/feedback/escalation paths.
8. Design wrong, incomplete, stale, unavailable, conflicting, timeout, and unsupported states.
9. For consequential actions, preview scope, require proportionate approval, and provide audit/rollback where feasible.
10. Test over-trust and under-trust scenarios.

## Agentic UX rules
Expose objective, material permissions, progress/state, sensitive action previews, cancellation, final result, exceptions, and unresolved items. Do not reveal hidden chain-of-thought; provide concise decision-relevant rationale and evidence.


## Transparency + accessibility
Check jurisdictional AI disclosure requirements; EU Article 50 may apply from 2026-08-02. Audit AI-generated content/UI after generation for semantics, reading order, status announcements, images/charts, correction and override.
