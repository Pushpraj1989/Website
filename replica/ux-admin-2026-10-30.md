# Staff console UX rework (2026-10-30)

**Scope:** the React staff console (`food-app/admin`), at phone width (390 px) and desktop width (1280 px).

**Classification:** professional-standard staff tool. It is used frequently, by staff of mixed experience, and its risk is moderate: it controls money-off rules, voucher codes and partner keys.

**Evidence:** E3 (rendered implementation). The screenshots in `ux-admin-2026-10-30/` come from the running console with the pilot data. Keyboard and screen-reader behaviour was not tested by hand, so accessibility claims stop at E3.

## Findings and fixes

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | S2 | Forms ran down the page: one full-width field per row. The campaign panel showed four forms open at once. | One grid for every form. Fields take the width their content needs, and short fields share a row (2 per row on a phone, 2–4 on desktop). "Add a rule", "New code pool", "Generate codes", "Add one code" and "Pictures and videos" now open on demand (native `details`/`summary`). A form opens by itself when it is the next thing to do, for example the first rule of an empty campaign. |
| 2 | S2 | On a phone the console was a shrunken web page. All 11 sections were stacked above the content, and tables scrolled sideways. | A top app bar, and a bottom tab bar for Orders, Menu, Campaigns and Reports. A "More" sheet opens from the bottom with all sections; it is a native `dialog`, so it traps focus and closes with Esc. Every table becomes a list of cards, with each value labelled by its column. The table roles are set explicitly, so screen readers keep the table structure. |
| 3 | S2 | On a phone, editors opened below a long list. | Editors open as full-screen sheets, with the title and close button pinned at the top and Save pinned at the bottom. The tab bar is hidden while a sheet is open. |
| 4 | S3 | A cancelled order showed in green, the colour for success. | Cancelled and rejected orders show in red. Delivered and collected orders are grey. Orders in progress stay green and new ones stay amber. The status is also written out in words, so the meaning doesn't rely on colour alone. |
| 5 | S3 | The per-customer limit could not be set from the console. | "Uses per customer" was added to the campaign rule form and the offer editor, and the server stores and checks it. It must be at least 1, and no more than the uses in total. A test was added. |
| 6 | S3 | A rule was shown as one line of text, so its limits could not be checked at a glance. | Each rule shows its settings as short labels: "10% off", "at most AED 20.00", "min order AED 30.00", "once per customer", "1 of 500 used". |
| 7 | S3 | The delivery areas page had unstyled buttons. The partner form's checkboxes were stretched full width. | The buttons use the console's button styles, and checkbox groups are laid out in columns. |
| 8 | S4 | "Optional" was written inside long labels. | It is now a small grey hint on the same line as the label. |
| 9 | S4 | Phones zoomed into fields with small text, and some controls were small to tap. | Field text is 16 px on a phone, and controls are at least 44 px tall. The layout respects the phone's safe areas. |

## Checks

- **No sideways scrolling:** none of the 11 sections scrolls sideways at 390 px or 1280 px (admin smoke test), and there are no page errors.
- **Full verification:** type check, 9 database suites (including the new per-customer limit test), the 42-check end-to-end API, the admin build, and the Flutter tests and analysis all pass.
- **Not yet done:** keyboard-only and screen-reader passes on a real phone (E4), and a usability check with restaurant staff.
