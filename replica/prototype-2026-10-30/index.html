What each part runs on

  • Hero slides: the hero promotions staff already publish in the admin, now full screen behind the address and search.
  • Offers carousel: live campaigns and shared codes, sliding on its own every few seconds.
  • Spotlight: the mid-feed promotion slot, with its picture or video.
  • Welcome offer: a new placement. Staff pick the offer, who sees it and how often; it shows once when the app opens.
  • Points and vouchers: the customer's membership points and saved vouchers.
  • Ratings: new. Customers rate a delivered order; a dish shows its score once it has enough ratings.
  • Offer strip: counts down the customer's best unused code.
