# Themes, 30 October 2026

A restaurant picks the look of its customer app and website in the admin (**Restaurant → Theme**). Its brand colour
goes on top of whichever theme it picks. Nothing about the look is built into the app.

| Theme | Look |
|---|---|
| Classic | Light: white cards on soft grey |
| Midnight | Dark: deep charcoal, for evening menus and bold food photos |
| Glass | Frosted, see-through cards over a soft wash of the brand colour |
| Glass night | Frosted dark cards over a glow of the brand colour |

Screenshots: `themes-2026-10-30/` (`app-home-all.png`, `app-menu-all.png`, `app-cart-all.png`, `web-menu-all.png`,
`web-checkout-all.png`, `admin-theme-picker.png`). Each sheet shows Classic, Midnight, Glass, Glass night from left to right.

## How it stays readable with any brand colour

- Buttons fill with the true brand colour. The admin only accepts colours with white text at 4.5:1 or more.
- Brand-coloured text, icons and outlines use a shade of the brand colour. That shade moves towards white (dark
  themes) or black (light themes) only as far as it must to reach 4.5:1 on every background it meets, including the
  brightest part of the glass backdrop.
- Text on brand tints, and on the green, amber and red notices, has its own ink colour in each theme.
- Red behind white text (a cancelled order) is the same deep red in every theme.

This is proven by calculation, not by eye. `mobile/test/theme_contrast_test.dart` and `web/test/theme.test.ts` try
every theme against hundreds of brand colours across the whole range the admin accepts. They check every text colour
on every background, in English and Arabic alike. Both run in `scripts/verify.sh`.

The proofs set two limits:

- The glass wash is held at 24% of the brand colour. At 26%, red error text fell to 4.46:1 with a black brand.
- The glass night glow is held at 38%. At 45%, muted text fell to 4.22:1 with a mid-grey brand.

## Where it lives

- Database: `restaurant.theme` (`db/branding.sql`), one of `classic`, `midnight`, `glass`, `glass_night`. Changes are audited.
- API: `PUT /v1/admin/restaurant` takes `theme`. The public brand (`/v1/brand`, `/v1/menu`) carries it.
- App: `AppTheme`, `Palette` and `BrandPalette` (`lib/core/theme.dart`). In the glass themes each page draws the
  backdrop, so pages stay solid while they slide. Cards, bars, the dock and the category bar blur what is behind them.
- Website: `lib/theme.ts` mirrors the app's colours and its contrast rule. The layout sets them as CSS variables.
  Before this change the website did not use the brand colour at all.
- Checks: the app sweep (`tool/audit_sweep_test.dart`) now runs every screen in every theme, with a deep navy brand
  colour, the hardest case on dark pages. The web audit passes in all four themes.
