# Experience review: customer app and admin console

Run with the Experience Architect skill pack (installed at `.claude/skills/`, in REVIEW mode with the screen review
protocol and the scoring gate). Date: 2026-10-29. Scores are estimates from the captures and automated checks listed
below. Nothing here has been user-tested or run with a screen reader.

## Verdict

**CONDITIONAL, both surfaces.** After round 2 the customer app scores **83/100** and the admin console **78/100**.
Round 1 scores were 76 and 76; the round 1 total for the customer app was printed as 78 and has been corrected here
(see "Score correction" below). Both are in the 75–84 band.

Neither surface can reach PASS yet, for two reasons:

1. No assistive-technology pass has run on a device. TalkBack and VoiceOver are not available in this environment, so
   screen-reader behaviour is `UNKNOWN`. The customer app has no keyboard pass at all.
2. Arabic has not been checked on a device, and the UAE regional rules have not been applied to the Arabic copy.

No hard-stop failure was found in the screens reviewed. Accessibility is below 12 on both surfaces, which blocks PASS
under the gate regardless of the other scores.

## Round 2: what changed

- **Bottom navigation (customer):** the signed-in app now has four destinations: Home, Menu, My orders, Membership.
- **Ask the restaurant (customer):** an icon in the app bar of Home and Menu. In round 2 it was first a floating button,
  which covered the Add button of the second popular dish. The final capture shows the Add buttons unobstructed.
- **Assistant empty state (customer):** an illustration, one line of guidance, and three example questions that fill
  the input when tapped.
- **Admin narrow layout:** sidebar stacks below 720 px; tables scroll inside their cards; page-level overflow is 0 px at
  390 px and 1280 px in the scan.
- **Admin names and focus:** every table region is focusable and named; the Products, Offers, Promotions, Categories,
  Orders and Zones tables have row and column headers; the Accept button names its order.

## What changed (design system)

- Illustrations: nine flat SVG files in `food-app/design/illustrations/`, plus `assistant.svg`, rendered to PNG for the
  app and copied to the admin.
- Category pictures are data, not code: the `categories.illustration` column (set in the admin) chooses which
  illustration a category shows. The app maps the key to a file.
- New tokens: accent soft background and three benefit tones. Hardcoded colours remain in the hero gradient and the
  membership tones; move them to tokens later.

## Acceptance tests for the next review

1. Customer app, keyboard or switch access: every control on the home, menu, cart and checkout is reachable in order,
   and each one has a visible focus state. **Not run.** The Flutter app has no keyboard harness.
2. Screen reader (TalkBack and VoiceOver): the Add buttons speak the dish name; the membership tiles say their title
   and summary; each order status is announced when it changes. **Not run.** No device in this environment. Automated
   semantics checks pass, which is evidence for names and sizes only.
3. Targets: every tappable control is at least 48 px. **Partly run:** the widget tests cover the Add buttons, icon
   buttons and bottom bar; the full customer app has not been measured on a device.
4. Arabic on a device: the whole flow reads right to left and no label is cut off. **Not run.** The captures show layout
   with a fallback font only.
5. Admin with keyboard only: sign in, move through the orders board, accept an order, and change a category picture.
   **Partly run:** the sidebar and tables were walked by keyboard and every stop was named and focusable. Accepting an
   order and changing a picture were not run.
6. Admin at 768 px wide: the product and order tables scroll sideways without losing the Accept button. **Not run** at
   768 px. 390 px and 1280 px pass the axe scan and show no page overflow.

## Method notes

- Customer captures: `food-app/mobile/tool/review_capture_test.dart` renders the customer screens into goldens. Run with
  `flutter test tool/review_capture_test.dart --update-goldens`. Arabic is rendered with FreeSerif as the fallback font
  in the capture only; the device uses the system Arabic font.
- Customer semantics: `food-app/mobile/test/semantics_test.dart`.
- Admin scan: `replica/review/tools/admin-a11y.mjs` (axe-core 4.14.0, 1280 and 390 px, seven pages); keyboard walk:
  `replica/review/tools/keyboard.mjs`; overflow finder: `replica/review/tools/find-page-wide.mjs`.
- Overflow at 390 px had no single source element. The admin's `.main` now clips horizontal overflow and card tables
  scroll inside their own regions. The clip contains the overflow; it is a mitigation, not a confirmed root-cause fix.
- The skill pack is in `.claude/skills/`: `experience-architect` (main) and six specialist skills.
