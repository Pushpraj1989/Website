## What changed (design system)

- Illustrations: nine flat SVG files in `food-app/design/illustrations/`, rendered to PNG for the app and copied to the
  admin.
- Category pictures are data, not code: the `categories.illustration` column (set in the admin) chooses which
  illustration a category shows. The app maps the key to a file.
- New tokens: accent soft background and three benefit tones. Hardcoded colours remain in the hero gradient and the
  membership tones; move them to tokens later.

## Acceptance tests for the next review

1. Customer app, keyboard or switch access: every control on the home, menu, cart and checkout is reachable in order,
   and each one has a visible focus state.
2. Screen reader (TalkBack and VoiceOver): the Add buttons speak the dish name; the membership tiles say their title
   and summary; each order status is announced when it changes.
3. Targets: every tappable control is at least 48 px (checked in the widget tests for the Add buttons only).
4. Arabic on a device: the whole flow reads right to left and no label is cut off.
5. Admin with keyboard only: sign in, move through the orders board, accept an order, and change a category picture.
6. Admin at 768 px wide: the product and order tables scroll sideways without losing the Accept button.
