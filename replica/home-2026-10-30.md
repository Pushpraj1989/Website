# The new home, built from the approved prototype (30 October 2026)

Everything a restaurant sees on the customer home is set in the admin. Nothing about the restaurant or its offers is
built into the app or the website.

## What staff control in the admin

**Promotions** (Admin → Promotions → Add promotion):

| Where it shows | What it is |
|---|---|
| Hero | Full width at the top of the home, behind the restaurant's name, address, icons and search. Slides change on their own. |
| Offers carousel | Cards that slide on their own, each in its own colour. |
| Pop-up when the app opens | The welcome offer, like "A little treat for you". |
| Offer strip | The slim line above the menu bar, while the cart is empty. |
| Spotlight | A large card between home sections. |
| Behind a product | A video behind a dish's page (unchanged). |

For each promotion:

- **Media:** upload a picture, an animated **GIF** or a video, reuse one from a product, or use none. The carousel, pop-up and strip can run on colour and words alone.
  - JPEG, PNG and WebP up to 3 MB.
  - GIF up to 8 MB.
  - MP4, MOV and WebM up to 60 MB.
  - Files are accepted by their real type, not their name.
- **Offer to show:** link one of the restaurant's offers. The pop-up then shows the offer's own terms, worked out from the offer: the headline (10% OFF), the code, the minimum spend, the largest saving, the last day and the days left. A promotion linked to an offer stops showing when the offer ends or is switched off.
- **How it arrives:** slide up from the bottom, down from the top, in from the left, in from the right, zoom in, or fade in.
- **Size:** compact, standard, tall or full screen. For the hero this is its height; for the pop-up, how much of the screen it covers.
- **Frame:** rounded card, square edges, ticket (with notches) or circle picture.
- **Colour:** behind white words. A colour too light for white words (below 4.5:1) is refused.
- **Seconds on screen:** for hero and carousel slides, 3 to 20.
- **How often the pop-up shows:** every time the app opens, once a day, or only once per customer.
- **Button opens:** the menu or one dish.

A small phone in the editor plays the chosen arrival, so staff see the effect before saving.

**Restaurant settings** (Admin → Restaurant):

- **Buttons:**
  - Light: white, with words in the brand colour. This is the default, and the calmest over photos.
  - Tonal: a soft tint.
  - Filled: the brand colour with white words.
  - The main action on a screen (Place order, Add to cart) stays filled in every style.
- **Icons:** in the brand colour, or in the text colour.
- **Ratings:** how many rated orders a dish needs before its stars show. The default is 5.

## What customers get

**App:**

- **Hero:** the hero photo runs under the status bar. The name, address, icons (Ask, language, cart with count, More) and search sit on it.
- **Below the hero:**
  - Categories.
  - Offers carousel.
  - Redeem and save: the customer's saved vouchers and membership.
  - Recommended dishes, with a small ADD button on each photo and the rating.
  - Spotlight.
  - Order again.
  - Explore more.
  - The menu.
  - The customer's own shortcuts.
  - A footer with "© year Restaurant name. All rights reserved." and the app version.
- **Bottom bar:** the menu bar stays pinned to the bottom of the screen while the page scrolls under it.
- **Slide-up cart:** after adding a dish, a bar shows the items and total. **View cart** slides the cart up from the bottom. There the customer changes amounts, chooses delivery or pickup, the address and how to pay, and places the order without leaving the screen. A dish's name opens its page; "See full cart" opens the cart screen for notes and codes.
- **Ratings:** after an order is delivered or collected, the customer rates it with stars (in Orders and on the order's page). A dish shows its average once enough orders are rated.
- **Assistant:**
  - Answers only about the restaurant's menu, the restaurant and the customer's own orders.
  - When a question is unclear it asks "About an order or about the menu?" with two buttons.
  - It follows the conversation.
  - When the customer asks for a dish, it offers it with the price for them to confirm; nothing is added until they tap **Add to cart**.
  - Every reply can be read aloud, or all replies can be read automatically.
- **About:** the restaurant's details, the app version, the copyright and the open-source notices.

**Website:**

- **Header:** icon buttons for the menu, the cart (with count) and the language, each with a spoken name and a tooltip.
- **Menu:** dishes show their rating, and the Add buttons follow the chosen style.
- **Welcome pop-up:** the same pop-up as the app, with the same arrival, size, frame and frequency (remembered in the browser).
- **Footer:** the restaurant's contact details, the copyright, the website version, and the staff sign-in link, which moves here from the header.

## Checked

- **Server tests:**
  - New home placements, GIF uploads, offer terms, and promotions stopping with their offer.
  - The look settings refusing bad values.
  - Ratings: owner only, after delivery, a minimum count before showing, re-rating replaces.
  - Button and icon styles.
  - The assistant's clarify choices, add-to-cart proposals (invented dishes and silly amounts dropped, prices from the database) and capped history.
- **App tests:**
  - The pop-up's frequency rules.
  - The assistant asking "order or menu" and sending the conversation.
  - Nothing added before the customer confirms.
  - The version shown matching the build.
- **App layout and accessibility sweep:** 197 runs over every screen, including the pop-up, the slide-up cart and About, at two widths, two text sizes, two languages and four themes. No findings other than the known Arabic-text contrast quirk of the check.
- **Website audit:** every theme with light and filled buttons, with the pop-up open, finds no problems.
- **Admin audit:** finds no problems.
- **`scripts/verify.sh`:** all 21 steps pass.

## Running it on staging

1. `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/pilot-setup.ts`: the pilot's offers. It prints a partner key once; store it at once.
2. `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/home-setup.ts`: a welcome pop-up, three carousel offers and an offer strip, built on those offers. Staff can change or replace all of it in the admin.
