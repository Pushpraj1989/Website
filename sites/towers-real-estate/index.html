FAQ

Questions people ask

Can't find an answer? Contact us.

Are the prices and availability on this site?

Not yet. The developer confirms prices and availability. Ask us and we will pass the question on.

Can foreigners buy property in Oman?

The published rules changed in 2026 (Decision No. 87/2026). Sources differ on which areas are open to foreign buyers. Please check with the Ministry of Housing and Urban Planning and your own adviser before you commit.

Does owning property give a residence visa?

Under the published framework, some owners of registered residential units can apply for a residence permit without a sponsor. Eligibility conditions apply, so please confirm them with an adviser.

Is this site an official developer site?

No. Towers Real Estate is a separate company. The renders are the developer's, credited on each image.
