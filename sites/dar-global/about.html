About Dar Global

Global capital. Enduring places.

Our story

Global capital. Enduring places.

DarGlobal develops real estate that speaks for itself, where lifestyle and value come together. We work with trusted global partners to create homes, communities, and destinations that grow in meaning and worth over time. Our approach is simple: deliver with integrity, design with care, and build with purpose. For those who see the world as their home, DarGlobal offers not just investment, but belonging - value you can measure, and pride you can feel, and a legacy you can pass forward.

A London-listed luxury real estate developer, investor, and asset manager creating high-quality communities, landmark destinations, and lifestyle-driven assets across premier global markets, including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Spain, and London, spanning premium hospitality, commercial, entertainment, and exclusive golf communities.

Our premium properties are strategically located in established and emerging markets. Creating opportunities to expand your world. While growing your wealth. Our international portfolio spans exquisite residences, vacation retreats, world-class hospitality, and developments with iconic luxury brands, including Aston Martin, and Lamborghini. Design. Lifestyle. Investment value. Every DarGlobal property seeks to enrich all three.

True global citizens want to live without borders. Or compromises. We offer thoughtfully designed homes in desirable settings that cater to your international way of life. Freedom and flexibility. Comfort and culture. Security and service. Wherever you go, find the fundamentals here.

We draw on over 31 years of luxury development experience to move with agility. Adapting to stay ahead of the market. Anticipating investors’ needs. Leveraging exceptional talent and partnerships. Always guided by purpose. Our commitment to quality and people-first thinking sets us apart. We don’t just build differently. We’re built different.
