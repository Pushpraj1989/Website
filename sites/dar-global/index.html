        FAQ

        Questions investors ask

        Can't find an answer? Contact us.

        Who is DarGlobal?

        DarGlobal develops real estate that speaks for itself, where lifestyle and value come together. We work with trusted global partners to create homes, communities, and destinations that grow in meaning and worth over time. Our approach is simple: deliver with integrity, design with care, and build with purpose. For those who see the world as their home, Dar Global offers not just investment, but belonging — value you can measure, and pride you can feel, and a legacy you can pass forward.

        Where does DarGlobal operate?

        Across 14 cities in 9 countries, including Spain, London, UAE, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Greece, the UK, and the Maldives.

        Can international buyers invest?

        Yes. DarGlobal is built for the global citizen — our destinations welcome international investors with full ownership rights, residency privileges, and secure, transparent investment frameworks.

        How does the buying process work?

        Select your property, consult our advisors, reserve securely, and complete your purchase digitally or through our global offices.

        What are the benefits of investing in off-plan luxury properties?

        Off-plan at DarGlobal means entering early into high-growth destinations, benefiting from attractive prices, flexible payment plans, and the appreciation that comes with world-class design and brand collaborations. It’s not just smart investing — it’s securing future value today.

        What are co-branded residences?

        These are homes crafted in collaboration with the world’s most iconic luxury brands — from fashion to hospitality to design — offering elevated architecture, exclusive services, and long-term value driven by global brand equity.

        What payment plans are available?

        Each development offers flexible installment plans, making global investing accessible while maximizing capital efficiency.

        Is DarGlobal a publicly listed company, and where is it traded?

        Yes, DarGlobal is publicly listed, offering investors the added confidence of regulated transparency and corporate governance.

        How does property registration work?

        Registration is completed with the respective land department or governing authority in each country. DarGlobal provides full guidance to ensure a smooth and compliant process.

        Is my investment with DarGlobal secure and transparent?

        Yes. With over three decades of delivery, DarGlobal is anchored in trust, clarity, and transparent processes. Every investor is protected by local regulations, escrow systems, and our global partnerships — ensuring peace of mind at every step.
