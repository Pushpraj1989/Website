        FAQ

        Questions people ask

        Can't find an answer? Contact us.

        Do you list calories and macros?

        Yes. Almost every dish on the menu shows its calories, protein, fat and carbohydrates, as written by our kitchen. Open any dish to see them.

        How can I pay?

        Online by debit or credit card through Thawani Pay, cash on delivery, or cash or card at the counter when you collect.

        Why do some dishes say the price depends on options?

        Those dishes, such as the lean burgers, buckets, juices and coffee, are priced by the options you choose. Ask us and we confirm the price before you order.

        How do meal plans work?

        Choose a goal, the size of the plan and the dishes you like. We call you to agree the menu, delivery days and price. The estimate on the page uses today's menu prices.

        Where are you?

        In Al Ghubra Ash Shamaliyyah, Muscat. Open the branch page for the map and directions.
