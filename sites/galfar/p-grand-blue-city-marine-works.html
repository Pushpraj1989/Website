Grand Blue City marine works

Marine works for a seafront community at Al Sawadi, South Al Batinah.

Ask about this project

Galfar was awarded the construction of marine works at Grand Blue City in Al Sawadi, South Al Batinah. The letter of award was approved by both parties on 23 June 2024.

The project is valued at RO 12 million with an execution period of 24 months. The development will include villas, lagoon villas, a five-star hotel, serviced apartments and 270 residential apartments along the Al Sawadi seafront.
