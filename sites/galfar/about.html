Our story

A partner in Oman's renaissance since 1972

Galfar Engineering & Contracting SAOG was established in 1972 and has grown into one of the largest publicly listed companies in Oman and one of the largest construction companies in the Middle East.

Its work runs through three business units: Energy & Industrial, Infrastructure, and Civil & Environmental, supported by units for QHSSE, project services, human capital, finance and IT, supply chain, and strategy and business development.

Galfar has delivered more than 1,000 projects worth over US$10 billion, built over 4,000 km of roads and laid over 8,000 km of pipelines and flowlines. It owns more than 4,000 plant and machines and works with over 1,500 registered local vendors.

With partners, Galfar has also moved into 3D printing for construction.
