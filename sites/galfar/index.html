        FAQ

        Questions people ask

        Can't find an answer? Contact us.

        When was Galfar founded?

        Galfar was established in 1972. Its first project was a villa for the Wali of Sur, and it obtained its commercial registration in 1975.

        Is Galfar a listed company?

        Yes. Galfar Engineering & Contracting SAOG has been listed on the Muscat Stock Exchange since 2007 under the ticker GECS, with more than 4,000 shareholders.

        What kind of projects does Galfar build?

        Three business units cover oil and gas, industrial and power projects; roads, bridges, airports, ports, dams, rail and water networks; and buildings, MEP and wastewater plants, including operation and maintenance.

        How do I register as a supplier?

        Vendors register through Galfar's Supply Chain Portal at gecs.galfar.com. More than 1,500 local vendors and suppliers are registered.

        How do I apply for a job at Galfar?

        Open roles are listed on Galfar's careers portal at galfar.zohorecruit.com.

        Where can I find the annual reports?

        Annual reports for 2021 to 2025 and ESG reports for 2024 and 2025 are published on the Investor Inquiries and Publications pages of galfar.com.

        Which standards is Galfar certified to?

        Galfar's QHSSE management system is certified to ISO 9001, ISO/TS 29001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001.

        How do I contact Galfar?

        Call the head office on +968 2452 5000 or email info@galfar.com. The postal address is Post Box 533, Muscat, Postal Code 100, Sultanate of Oman.
