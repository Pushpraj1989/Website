About

Multi-disciplined, from design to operation

Galfar has been established in Oman since 1972. Its work spans engineering, procurement and construction for energy and industrial plants, buildings and environmental works, and roads, airports, seaports, dams and water networks, including operation and maintenance.

  • Operation & maintenance

    Galfar also operates and maintains several of the facilities it builds.

  • Quality, safety & environment

    QHSSE is one of the group's dedicated support units.

  • Iconic projects

    Yibal Khuff, Salalah Airport, the Boushar-Amirat Road and Duqm Fishery Port.
