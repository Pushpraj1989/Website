Render: Starcare facilities gallery

Overview

The emergency department at Starcare hospital is manned by ACLS certified doctors and nursing staff who are capable of managing all types of emergencies. The department follows internationally accepted ER protocols.

The emergency department is a 24-hour fully-equipped medical facility, skilled to provide immediate pain relief, stabilizing the patients, and is the place where you can get immediate medical attention when it is crucially needed.

Services offered

  • Chest pain / Myocardial infarction
  • Difficulty in breathing / asthma attacks / pneumonia
  • Pulmonary embolism ( Blockage of a major blood vessel in the lung)
  • Unconsciousness
  • Altered sensorium (inability to think or concentrate clearly)
  • Food/drug poisoning or overdose
  • Hematemesis (Blood Vomiting)
  • Hemoptysis ( Blood when coughing)
  • Anaphylactic reaction (Allergy)
  • Seizures (sudden behavioral change due to abnormal electrical activity in the brain)
  • Trauma
  • Fractures
  • Acute abdominal emergencies
  • Minor burns
  • Psychiatric cases
  • Renal colic (blockage in the urinary tract)
