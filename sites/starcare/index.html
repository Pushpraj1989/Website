        FAQ

        Questions patients ask

        Can't find an answer? Contact us.

        Is Starcare accredited?

        Yes. Starcare holds JCI, PSFHI and ISO accreditation, which made it the first triple-accredited hospital in Oman. JCI re-accredited Starcare for the fourth time in August 2021.

        Is there a 24-hour emergency department?

        Starcare Hospital Seeb runs a 24-hour emergency department staffed by ACLS-certified doctors and nurses. Call +968 2455 7200 or +968 9808 8705.

        Which insurance do you accept?

        Starcare works with most insurers and TPAs for cashless or direct billing, including Aetna, AXA, Bupa, Cigna, MedNet, MSH International, NEXtCARE, SAICO, Vipul BetterCare and MetLife. Starcare Barka also accepts National Life & General Insurance cards. Check your policy with the branch before your visit.

        How do I book an appointment?

        Call your branch, or use the form on this site. Starcare Barka also takes appointment requests by WhatsApp on +968 9331 7359.

        What does the 24 to 30 week antenatal package include?

        Nine consultations with a consultant obstetrician, two ultrasound scans, two full blood counts and a dipstick urine test at each visit. Other tests, admissions and extra consultations are charged separately.

        What is in the normal delivery package?

        A planned, uncomplicated delivery with 1 night and 2 days for mother and baby in a standard room, meals, a companion, routine tests, the baby's BCG and hepatitis B vaccines, a hearing screen and follow-ups with the paediatrician and obstetrician within two weeks.

        Which check-up should I choose?

        It depends on your sex, age and lifestyle risk factors. Call +968 9808 8705 or +968 2455 7200 and the team will suggest one.

        Where are Starcare branches?

        Hospitals in Seeb and Barka, medical centres in Mabela, Bousher and Duqm, a clinic in Saha (Samail), and branches in Rolla, Sharjah and in Calicut, India.
