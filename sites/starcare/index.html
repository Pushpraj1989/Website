About

Eight sites, one hospital group

Starcare operates six sites in Oman, one in Sharjah in the UAE, and one listed in Calicut, India. Each site lists its own address and phone number.

  • Workforce

    Over 500 doctors, nurses, paramedics and support staff, according to the group's site.

  • In-house pharmacy

    The pharmacy stocks a range of lifesaving medicines.

  • Check-ups & packages

    Health check packages are offered across the group.
