Lubricants

HP Lubricants

Automotive, industrial and speciality oils and greases from HPCL, marketed in Oman by National Gas.

Order lubricants

National Gas offers HP Lubricants, 'Power to Perform', from HPCL, one of the world's largest lube marketers. HPCL owns one of the largest lube base-oil refineries and makes more than 450 grades of lubricants, greases and specialities.

The range covers transport, railways, manufacturing, mining, shipping, agriculture and power. National Gas launched its lubricants division in 2016 and HP Lubricants in Oman in September 2020.

Key facts

Automotive
Car, motorcycle and diesel engine oils, gear and transmission oils, greases
Industrial
Hydraulic, compressor, turbine, gear and transformer oils, greases
Specialities
Quenching oils, thermic fluids, rust preventives, cutting fluids
