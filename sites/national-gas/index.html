About

Built around LPG supply

National Gas Company runs LPG plants and storage in Oman, the UAE and Malaysia. Its subsidiaries operate in the UAE, Malaysia and Saudi Arabia.

  • Plants

    Eleven LPG plants across Oman, the UAE and Malaysia.

  • Storage

    2,600 tonnes of LPG storage, which the group describes as the largest in Oman.

  • Throughput

    More than 350,000 metric tonnes of LPG a year.
