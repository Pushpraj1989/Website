From 1981

In the late 1970s Omani dealers drove to Dubai and Abu Dhabi for gas cylinders. In 1981 National Gas started bottling at home.

Refinery to burner

How LPG reaches you

Lifted at the refinery

Our tankers collect bulk LPG from refineries in the Sultanate. Every vehicle carries tracking and online monitoring, and drivers train regularly.

02 · 2,600 t

Stored in bulk

About 2,600 tonnes of bulk storage, the largest in Oman, spread across strategic points in the Sultanate.

03 · Since 1981

Bottled and sealed

Plants fill each cylinder to the right quantity in controlled conditions, and every one leaves with the Silver Seal.

Delivered by road

        FAQ

        Questions people ask

        Can't find an answer? Contact us.

        What is LPG?

        LPG stands for liquefied petroleum gas. It is a mixture of propane and butane, compressed and stored as a liquid for use as fuel.

        Where does LPG come from?

        LPG is a by-product of oil and natural gas production, and is also extracted at refineries and gas processing plants.

        How do I buy LPG from National Gas?

        National Gas supplies LPG cylinders through its distributor network, and delivers bulk LPG and D-Cylinder refills directly to commercial and industrial customers. Call +968 2416 2646 or write to sales@nationalgasco.net.

        How can I pay?

        Online, at company outlets, or by bank transfer.

        What should I do if I smell gas?

        Open all doors and windows fully. Do not switch lights, fans or any appliance on or off. Close the cylinder valve and switch off the regulator, and put out any flame or cigarette.

        How do I check a cylinder for leaks?

        Use a sponge or brush with soapy water on the joints. Never use a match or a flame. If there is a leak, close the valve and do not use the cylinder until an expert has repaired it.

        Why is LPG heavier than air a safety point?

        LPG vapour is 1.5 to 2 times heavier than air, so a leak collects at ground level. Keep cylinders upright, out of the sun and outside the kitchen, never in a pit or basement.

        What is a D-Cylinder?

        A fixed 440-litre LPG cylinder designed by National Gas for hotels, restaurants and factories. A bobtail fills it on site and you are billed for the metered quantity.
