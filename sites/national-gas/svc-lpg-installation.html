LPG installation

Bulk LPG systems from 3,000 to 300,000 litres, designed, fabricated and commissioned by our engineers.

Photo: National Gas Company

Overview

National Gas has installed some of the largest LPG systems in Oman and the GCC, from 3,000 to 300,000 litres.

The team takes projects from design and fabrication to commissioning, and then keeps them supplied: bulk storage at strategic points across the Sultanate and the largest fleet of bulk tankers and bobtails in Oman.

We have designed, installed and commissioned LPG systems for poultry, glass, ceramic and powder plants across the GCC, and our engineers advise customers on running installations at international standards of safety and efficiency.

Why LPG

  • Clean burn, no carbon deposits
  • No spillage, no fumes
  • No pilferage from a closed, pressurised system
  • Higher efficiency than diesel

3,000 to 300,000 L
