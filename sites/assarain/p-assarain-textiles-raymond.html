Assarain Textiles (Raymond)

Raymond menswear and tailoring in Oman, with a showroom on Ruwi High Street. Established in 1990.

Contact the Group

Giorgio Armani once said, "Elegance is not standing out, but being remembered." Assarain Textiles LLC, in collaboration with the prestigious Raymond Limited of India, is always fresh in the memory of our valued customers for bringing in a complete range of premier men's clothing to the Sultanate of Oman and for catering to their impeccable sense of dressing with elegance and style. Established in 1990, we have always been offering the latest in global fashion trends by way of an affordable, practical and trusted clothing line that includes a wide spectrum of menswear. From exclusive formals to relaxed casuals, our Raymond showroom on Ruwi High Street is an established entity of high quality suits, shirts, trousers and fabrics, all bearing the world-renowned Raymond trademark of unsurpassed standards.

At Assarain Textiles, we have a diverse product range of many designs and colours of fabric and textiles to suit every age, occasion and style. Holding our own in-house design, fabrication and tailoring divisions, we employ a team of specialized master tailors and designers, each member exclusively trained by Raymond Limited fashion experts in India. Our employment of such finesse in expertise and execution, has ensured that our company also provides a wide range of uniforms for several airlines, hotels and sales personnel within Oman's booming service industry. Through our top-notch product range, we have always set new benchmarks for excellence in the Omani textile industry.

Our innovative designs are infused with superior quality and service, all backed by expert professionals: a simple formula that has allowed Assarain Textiles LLC to flourish over the past two and a half decades, and that will allow us to continue to grow hand in hand with the Sultanate of Oman.
