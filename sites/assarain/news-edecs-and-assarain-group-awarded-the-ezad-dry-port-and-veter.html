EDECS and Assarain Group awarded the EZAD dry port and veterinary quarantine

OPAZ awarded the dry port and veterinary quarantine at the Economic Zone at Al Dhahirah to EDECS, with Assarain Group as local partner.

Still from the Assarain corporate film

The Public Authority for Special Economic Zones and Free Zones (OPAZ) awarded EDECS the development of a dry port and veterinary quarantine at the Economic Zone at Al Dhahirah (EZAD) in Ibri. Assarain Group, also known as Said Salem Al Wahaibi Group, is the local partner in Oman.

The zone covers about 388 km² and lies roughly 20 km from the Rub Al Khali border crossing. Press coverage quoted Assarain Group Chairman Sheikh Salem bin Said Al Wahaibi.

Construction Business News Middle East, Container News and Gulf Construction also reported the award in May 2026.
