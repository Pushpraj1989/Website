Alserkal & Assarain Concrete Products (AACP)

Dubai joint venture with Nasser Bin Abdullatif Alserkal Est.: pavers, blocks, kerbs and slabs for the UAE.

Contact the Group

Alserkal & Assarain Concrete Products (AACP) is a joint venture between Assarain Concrete Products & Trading LLC, Oman & M/s Nasser Bin Abdullatif Alserkal Est, UAE.

We offer a diverse range of interlock pavers, concrete blocks, hydraulically pressed kerbstones, custom kerbs, paving slabs, roof slabs, cable tiles and covers for a variety of applications in both urban and interior areas in the UAE.

Catering to various Dubai Government Bodies, contracting companies and specialized clientele, we have been market leaders for over three decades, implementing state-of-the-art technology and experience to offer a multitude of innovative, high quality products. We also offer attractive landscaping products, creatively designed planters, benches, litter bins, park equipment and much more as part of the iconic backdrop for the UAE.

We utilize an immense, high capacity, advanced German plant to manufacture these superior quality concrete products. The plant is equipped with a MASA 9001XL Block Manufacturing System for the production of high density concrete blocks. These include Hollow, Solid, Hourdi and Insulated Blocks, as well as high density, abrasion-resistant interlocking paving tiles. Our manufacturing facility is equipped to produce up to 7 million masonry blocks and 1.5 million square meters of interlocking tiles per year, all within a wide variety of colours, sizes, shapes, finishes and textures.

We are proud to be recognized as one of the few concrete product suppliers with Integrated Management System (IMS) Certification. Reflecting international recognition for our management procedures, processes and customer focus, the IMS Certification represents the way we prioritize Quality, Environmental and Occupational Health & Safety Systems and ensure that we implement and sustain them in all processes throughout our organization. These measures, which outline stringent quality standards, ensure that our products align with the requirements in place for Dubai's superior levels of design and construction.

In addition to this, the Dubai Central Laboratories Department (DCLD) has accredited our AACP Laboratory Facilities as a self-certifying company, the first of its kind with such a distinction. Proud to be a leader and a favoured company, our organization Alserkal & Assarain Concrete Products is continually reinforcedwith the relentless supply of new ideas, technology and initiatives to not merely the UAE and the Middle East Region, but also the rest of the world.
