Where ACP began

        FAQ

        Questions about the Group

        Can't find an answer? Contact us.

        What is the Assarain Group?

        A Muscat-based trading and investment group, founded in 1975 and part of the Said Salem Al Wahaibi (SSW) Group of Companies.

        How many companies are in the Group?

        assarain.com says 19+. Its English pages list 17 companies across four divisions, and its Arabic pages list four more: Strabag Oman, Blue Falcon Travel, Wadi Adai Auto Service Centre and Al Haq Trading.

        Which sectors does it work in?

        Manufacturing (concrete, steel, plastics), construction, maintenance and property, retail and consumer services (furnishing, insurance, beauty retail, healthcare, menswear, facility services) and food distribution.

        Where does the Group operate?

        Across Oman, with offices and plants in Muscat, Rusayl, Sohar and Salalah, and in the UAE through Alserkal & Assarain Concrete Products in Dubai.

        How do I contact the head office?

        Call +968 2460 4811 or email business@assarain.com. The postal address is PO Box 1475, PC 112, Ruwi, Muscat.

        How do I apply for a job?

        Use the Apply Online form on assarain.com: name, position, phone, email, years of experience and your CV. No vacancies are listed on the site.
