About

Five decades of diversified growth

Assarain has operated since its inception in 1975. Its companies sit in four business groups: retail and consumer services, commercial wholesale, manufacturing, and construction, maintenance and property.

  • Joint ventures

    Savills and Partners Oman, and Kone Assarain LLC.

  • Manufacturing base

    Concrete products, modern steel mills, and plastic and acrylic industries.

  • Health & insurance

    Hatat Specialty Clinics and Assarain Insurance Services are part of the group.
