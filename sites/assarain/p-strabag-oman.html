Strabag Oman

Partnership with STRABAG for roads, bridges, airports, dams and ports. Founded in 1971.

Contact the Group

Strabag Oman LLC is a partnership between the Assarain Group and STRABAG. Its Arabic company page lists work on roads, bridges, airports, dams, commercial complexes and ports.

Projects named on the page include the Nizwa to Ibri highway, the Sur suspension bridge, an air base, a dam in Salalah, airport rehabilitation, the Al Multaqa training centre, the Thumrait road and dualisation of the Sohar road.

The company is ISO 9001 certified. This summary is translated from the Arabic page on assarain.com; the company is not listed in the English navigation.
