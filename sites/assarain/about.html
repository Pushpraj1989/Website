About Assarain

Trust is our capital

Our story

Trust is our capital

The Assarain Group, part of the Said Salem Al Wahaibi (SSW) Group of Companies, is a trading and investment organisation based in Muscat. Since its inception in 1975 it has been part of business development across the Sultanate of Oman.

Its companies work in construction, manufacturing, national infrastructure, consumer products, food distribution, insurance and healthcare, and the Group has entered strategic collaborations with global companies including KONE, Savills, The Body Shop, Raymond, STRABAG and Alserkal.

The Group says most of its business entities hold ISO certifications, and that it remains dedicated to vertical and lateral progress, innovation, customer orientation and total quality management, and Omanization alongside global collaboration.

Recent press names Sheikh Salem bin Said Al Wahaibi as Chairman of Assarain Group. The founder, Colonel Said bin Salem Al Wahaibi, passed away in October 2020.

Milestones

How we got here

  1. 1975

    Assarain Group founded

    Assarain Furnishing and Blue Falcon Travel were established the same year.

  2. 1984

    Assarain Concrete Products

    The future flagship company starts making concrete products.

  3. 1990

    Hatat clinics and Raymond

    Hatat Specialty Clinics and Assarain Textiles open.

  4. 1994

    AICC becomes independent

    Assarain International Contracting is set up as its own company.

  5. 1995

    Properties and The Body Shop

    Hay Al Rahbah (Assarain Properties) and NMTC are established.

  6. 2001

    Steel and plastics

    Modern Steel Mills at Rusayl and Shamal Plastic in Sohar.

  7. 2012

    KONE Assarain

    Joint venture with KONE for elevators and escalators.

  8. 2014

    ACP award

    Best Manufacturer in Construction Material, Concrete Products.

  9. 2018

    Duqm

    ACP signs a 25-year lease for a 60,000 m² plot in Duqm.

  10. 2025

    EDECS partnership

    Strategic partnership signed in Muscat in February.

  11. 2026

    EZAD dry port

    EDECS and Assarain awarded the dry port and veterinary quarantine.
