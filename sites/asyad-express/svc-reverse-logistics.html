Reverse Logistics

Doorstep pickup, quality checks and restocking of returns across the GCC.

Reverse logistics campaign

Overview

An end-to-end returns service launched in July 2025 for retailers and digital-first brands.

With GCC-wide coverage, returns are picked up from the customer's door, checked and put back into inventory, with tracking for the whole cycle.

The service plugs into existing e-commerce and retail workflows for SMEs and large enterprises alike.

Features

  • Doorstep pickup
  • Quality checks
  • Reintegration into inventory
  • Tracking throughout the return
  • GCC-wide coverage
