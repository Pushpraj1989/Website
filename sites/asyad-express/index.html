How a parcel moves

From order to door

01

An order comes in

A seller takes an order online, in the client portal or by phone, and books it with Asyad Express.

02

Stock waits on the racks

Fulfilment customers keep inventory in the warehouse near Muscat International Airport, tracked by a management system.

03

Picked and packed

Orders are picked, packed in Asyad boxes or flyers and labelled.

04

Sorted for its route

Labelled parcels are sorted for domestic and international routes.

05

Out through the dock

Seven dock doors load the vans and trucks. International shipments clear customs in-house.

06

On the road

Same day in Muscat, up to 72 hours to remote areas, 24 to 48 hours to GCC cities.

07

At the door

The courier collects cash or card on delivery and the sender gets online proof of delivery.

Fulfilment and warehousing

A warehouse with airside access

The Asyad Express warehouse sits at Muscat International Airport, with a bonded goods area, a customs office and x-ray machines to speed up inspection and clearance.

  • More than 3,500 m² of warehousing
  • Over 1,200 m² of fulfilment space
  • Seven dock doors, three dock levellers
  • 24-hour security and CCTV
  • ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certified

        FAQ

        Questions people ask

        Can't find an answer? Contact us.

        How long will the shipment take to be delivered?

        Asyad Express's FAQ says two days within Muscat Governorate and three days to other regions, with deliveries to Musandam once a week. In December 2025 the company described same-day delivery within Muscat and up to 72 hours nationwide.

        Can I reschedule the delivery time?

        Yes. Call the Asyad Express call centre on 1001 to reschedule your delivery.

        What is Asyad Express?

        Asyad Express provides pick-up and delivery for businesses of all types in Oman, from growing SMEs to international companies in e-commerce, manufacturing, services and finance.

        What is Asyad Express fulfilment?

        A dedicated warehouse space for receiving inventory, managing storage and completing orders for businesses, including picking, packing, labelling, dispatch and delivery with full tracking.

        How much can I send abroad?

        International Express takes documents and merchandise up to 30 kg to more than 220 destinations. Price is based on actual or volumetric weight (L × W × H ÷ 5000), whichever is higher.

        Can I insure an international shipment?

        Yes. Insurance costs 2.5% of the shipment value, and the full value is refunded in case of loss or damage.

        How do I reach the sales team?

        Fill in the contact form with your details and the sales team will reply within two working days.

        What if my shipment is damaged, wrong or missing?

        Send the details through the contact form and customer service will reply within two working days.
