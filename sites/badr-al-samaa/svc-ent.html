ENT

Ear, nose and throat

Photo: Badr Al Samaa Group

Overview

The ENT (Ear, Nose & Throat) Department at Badr Al Samaa provides comprehensive diagnostic, medical, and surgical care for disorders affecting the ear, nose, throat, head, and neck. Supported by experienced ENT specialists and advanced diagnostic technology, the department delivers personalized treatment for patients of all age groups, from children to adults.

Our specialists are experienced in managing a wide range of ENT conditions, including sinusitis, allergic rhinitis, nasal obstruction, hearing loss, ear infections, vertigo, tinnitus, tonsillitis, adenoid disorders, voice and swallowing problems, snoring, sleep-related breathing disorders, and head and neck conditions. We also provide specialized care for paediatric ENT disorders and hearing-related concerns in children.

The department offers advanced surgical services including endoscopic sinus surgery, microscopic ear surgery, tonsillectomy, adenoidectomy, septoplasty, nasal and airway procedures, and other minimally invasive ENT surgeries. Using modern endoscopic and microscopic techniques, our specialists focus on achieving effective treatment outcomes with enhanced patient comfort and faster recovery.

We also provide hearing assessment and screening services, including neonatal hearing screening, audiological evaluations, and support for patients with hearing impairment. Speech and voice-related disorders are managed through comprehensive assessment and treatment plans aimed at improving communication and quality of life.

Equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic and surgical facilities, the ENT Department at Badr Al Samaa Hospital is committed to delivering high-quality, patient-centred care for both routine and complex ear, nose, throat, and head & neck conditions.
