About

From one polyclinic in Ruwi to a GCC group

The venture was started off in Ruwi, Oman, by a group of renowned visionaries in 2002, aiming to provide world-class multispecialty healthcare at affordable costs.

Made its humble beginning as Polyclinic in 2002 and gradually became the largest private healthcare group of Oman. The group spread its wings and became multinational, having presence in Oman, Bahrain, UAE, Kuwait and Qatar.

        FAQ

        Questions patients ask

        Can't find an answer? Contact us.

        How do I book an appointment?

        Use the online form, call the 24/7 helpline on +968 2271 7171, or message the team on WhatsApp at +968 9393 2255. Choose your branch and a coordinator confirms the slot.

        Is emergency care available around the clock?

        Yes. Emergency care is staffed 24x7 by in-house physicians, trauma surgeons and nurses, with ambulances, portable life support, blood bank and laboratory services. Ambulance: +968 2479 9760.

        Which insurance do you accept?

        The group works with the region's major insurers on a cashless basis, including NEXtCARE, MetLife, GIG, Cigna, Bupa, Allianz Worldwide Care, Oman Insurance Company and National Health Insurance.

        How much is a health check-up?

        Packages in Oman start at OMR 35 for the Executive Body Check Up and go up to OMR 115 for the Complete Heart & Body Check Up. Prices for Bahrain, Kuwait, UAE and Qatar are on the packages page.

        Can I see a doctor by video?

        Yes. Video consultations can be booked online with doctors at each branch.

        Do you help international patients?

        The medical tourism team arranges visa processing for patients and relatives, flights and airport transfers, and accommodation with dietary needs.

        Which countries do you operate in?

        Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait and the UAE, with hospitals, medical centres and polyclinics in each.
