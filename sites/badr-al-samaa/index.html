About

From one polyclinic in Ruwi to a GCC group

Badr Al Samaa began in 2002 as a polyclinic in Ruwi. It has grown into a group of hospitals, medical centres and clinics across Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait and the UAE.

  • Centres of excellence

    Cardiology, orthopaedics, obstetrics & gynaecology, urology & andrology, gastroenterology, radiology, ENT and dentistry.

  • Preventive health

    Check-up packages for asthma and allergy, diabetes, blood pressure and thyroid.

  • Insurance & medical tourism

    Both are listed among the group's services.
