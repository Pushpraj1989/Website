How we build a Spanish latte

Five layers, poured in this order, every time.

  1. Double-walled glassKeeps it hot, shows the layers
  2. Cinnamon dustA light finish
  3. MicrofoamA thin, glossy cap
  4. Double espresso18 g in, 36 g out, 28 seconds
  5. Steamed whole milkSilky, 60 to 65 °C
  6. Condensed milkA spoon of sweetness at the base
