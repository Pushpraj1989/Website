How we build a Spanish latte

Five layers, poured in this order, every time.

  1. Double-walled glassKeeps it hot, shows the layers
  2. Cinnamon dustA light finish
  3. MicrofoamA thin, glossy cap
  4. Double espresso18 g in, 36 g out, 28 seconds
  5. Steamed whole milkSilky, 60 to 65 °C
  6. Condensed milkA spoon of sweetness at the base

        FAQ

        Questions people ask

        Can't find an answer? Contact us.

        Can I order ahead?

        Yes. Use the order form and pick a café and a time. This is a demo, so the form is not connected.

        Do you charge for plant milk?

        No. Oat, almond and lactose-free milk cost the same as whole milk.

        Do you sell beans?

        Yes. Four origins in 250 g bags and the house blend in 1 kg, roasted every Tuesday.

        Do you cater events?

        Yes, from 30 guests, with a barista, an espresso machine and pastries.
